I

The Maison

Luxury isn't announced. It's felt.

Lujuria was born in southern Spain, in workshops where gold is shaped by hand and the afternoon light decides which stone deserves to shine first. We don't design jewelry to be seen — we design it to be remembered by whoever wears it.

Every collection is a love letter to Spanish goldsmithing: filigree passed down through generations, stones chosen one by one, and a name that needs no permission to be desired.

Heritage

Designed by TUSDIO, crafted in Spain.

Lujuria's identity fuses contemporary aesthetics with the tradition of Spain's master goldsmiths. Every line of the logotype, every tone in the palette, answers to one idea: exclusivity isn't bought — it's inherited, or earned.

TUSDIO stands beside Lujuria at every branding decision — from the box that wraps the ring, to the pixel that presents it on screen.
